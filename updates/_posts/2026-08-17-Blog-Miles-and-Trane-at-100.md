Inspired by the centennial anniversary for both Miles Davis and John Coltrane, Semantic Lab co-director Matt Miller explores how preliminary tests of an expanded corpus of Linked Jazz materials illuminate over 500 new relationships between the musicians. Find the post, *[Miles & Trane at 100 Exploring the Davis and Coltrane centennial with Linked Jazz,]((https://semlab.io/blog/davis-coltrane-centennial.html))* on the Semantic Lab's blog, *[Infinite Semiosis.](https://semlab.io/blog/)*
