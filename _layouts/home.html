About

The Semantic Lab is a collective of researchers, students, and practitioners exploring new forms and functions of semantic technologies for libraries, archives, and museums.

Based at Pratt Institute’s School of Information, the Lab is led by Professor M. Cristina Pattuelli, along with Matt Miller, Adjunct Professor and Linked Data Applications Technical Specialist at the Library of Congress.
